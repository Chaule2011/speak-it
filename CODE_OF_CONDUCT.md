# Code of Conduct

Speak It welcomes contributors of every background and skill level.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests, and discussions.
- Explain disagreements with the work rather than attacking people.
- Respect privacy; never publish another person's contact details or credentials.
- Help keep examples and classroom material appropriate for learners.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, deliberate disruption,
and disclosure of private information are not permitted.

## Enforcement

The project maintainer may remove inappropriate contributions and restrict
participation. Enforcement should be proportionate: a correction or warning
for minor incidents, and temporary or permanent exclusion for serious or
repeated incidents. These rules apply to project issues, pull requests,
discussions, and other official project spaces.

For public incidents, use GitHub's report controls. For confidential reports,
use GitHub's private abuse-reporting tools; do not post sensitive details in an
issue. Maintainers should protect the privacy of everyone involved.
